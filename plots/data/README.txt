There are two types of files: 1) "DeltasFull.." and 2) "DeltasError..". The first one contains the actual deltas for each interaction, while the second one contains the absolute residuals. They are organized as follows:

1) Energy   K   Kinv   S   Sinv   T   Tinv   hadamard*-30   hadamard*-60   hadamard*-90   Mix   Exact

2) Energy   K   Kinv   S   Sinv   T   Tinv   hadamard*-30   hadamard*-60   hadamard*-90   Mix
